# Continue authorized work

Follow the [Clemora workflow adapters](../../shared/skill-adapters.md) and [operating contract](../../shared/agent-workflow.md).

Make reasonable routine implementation choices, state material assumptions, and continue work within the user's authorized outcome. Prepare a concrete reviewable result before requesting a final approval that is actually required.

Ask when an unresolved rule changes permissions, scope, data handling, business behavior, or the expected result. Continue independent work while waiting. Reversibility does not authorize unrelated edits, external messages, production access, force pushes, destructive data changes, merges, or deployments.

Clemora's open quotation, availability, and confirmation rules remain decisions for the user. Do not silently choose them to stay unblocked, and do not re-ask permission for work already authorized.
