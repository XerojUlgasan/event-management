# Humanize text

Polish existing English prose so it is clear, natural, and professional while preserving meaning, evidence, citations, and the author's voice. Use for humanize, less stiff, clearer wording, or paper-polishing requests.

Use this for an existing English draft. For a new document or substantive research work, start with [docs](docs.md) or [research-desk](research-desk.md). Use the [documentation agent](../AGENT.md) when the task spans files or a paper.

## Preserve the argument

- Read the section and enough surrounding text to understand its purpose and voice. Preserve the degree of formality required by the audience or institutional template.
- Keep factual meaning, technical terms, numbers, units, citations, scope boundaries, and uncertainty intact. Keep citations attached to the claims they support.
- Preserve direct quotations and required template language verbatim. Flag a substantive ambiguity or unsupported claim for research review instead of masking it with fluent prose.
- Do not introduce new findings, benefits, comparisons, personal experiences, or certainty during a wording pass. Maintain the distinction between proposed, implemented, tested, and observed behavior.

## Improve the prose

Replace vague abstractions and inflated phrasing with concrete nouns and verbs. Remove repetition, filler, canned transitions, and sentences that add no meaning. Vary sentence length when it improves flow; keep paragraphs centered on one idea and make their connection explicit. Use active voice when it clarifies responsibility without changing the claim.

Retain the author's terminology and natural professional voice. Do not force a casual tone, add anecdotes, introduce deliberate errors, or promise AI-detector outcomes. This workflow improves writing quality, not evidence or authorship status.

## Check the revision

Compare the original and revision for meaning, omissions, citation placement, numbers, technical distinctions, and confidence. Read the surrounding passage again for transitions and consistency. For a file-based request, edit the authorized artifact and run the [recursive review](recursive-review.md) across affected sections.

For pasted text, return the revised text directly with a brief note only when a substantive uncertainty remains. For non-English text, preserve it and route translation or editing according to the user's request rather than silently translating it.
