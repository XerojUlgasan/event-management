# Research superpowers

Coordinate a methodology-aware research workflow across idea, validation, literature, source extraction, analysis, drafting, and review. Use for multi-stage paper work; route a single-stage request directly to its specialist workflow.

This is a local research coordinator adapted from the supplied workflow summary. Use the documentation workflows and available tools; do not assume Claude Code/Cowork, a catalog plugin, or a search MCP is installed.

## Establish the current stage

Read the [documentation agent](../AGENT.md), the current manuscript, research questions/objectives, available evidence, and relevant requirements. Identify the stage, intended methodology, and requested deliverable. Enter at the current stage rather than restarting an approved topic.

For multi-session work, maintain the draft's compact research ledger: questions, stage status, sources and accessible evidence, claim locations, methods/analysis decisions, and unresolved dependencies. Record what was actually completed.

## Coordinate the necessary stages

| Stage | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Idea and scope | Use [design-it-capstones](design-it-capstones.md) when shaping a new idea; preserve an approved client and project boundary. |
| Viability | Use [validate-it-capstones](validate-it-capstones.md) to stress-test the idea or a material scope revision. |
| Literature and ingestion | Use [research-desk](research-desk.md). Extract claims relevant to the project's questions, methods, comparison criteria, and limitations, with source locations. |
| Methods and analysis | Match the design and analysis to the research question and available data. Record assumptions and reproducible analysis steps; distinguish planned analysis from executed results. |
| Draft | Use [docs](docs.md), preserving traceability from question and evidence through methods, results, and conclusions. |
| Review and revision | Use [research-desk](research-desk.md) for substantive review, fix supported issues, then [humanize-text](humanize-text.md) and the recursive document checks. |
| Defense or submission | Use [thesis-examiner](thesis-examiner.md) for requested defense preparation. Check the actual venue/template requirements if submission preparation is requested. |

## Match the method

- **Interpretive/hermeneutic or qualitative:** preserve context, provenance, interpretive decisions, contradictory evidence, and the basis for themes. Do not turn interview themes into prevalence claims without supporting sampling and measurement.
- **Quantitative:** identify variables, population/sample, instruments, missing-data treatment, and an appropriate analysis. Report actual denominators, uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations; do not infer causation from a descriptive design.
- **Mixed methods:** state how the qualitative and quantitative parts connect, where integration occurs, and how conflicting findings will be handled. Do not label two unrelated data collections as an integrated design.

When required data or an approved research decision is missing, surface the dependency and continue independent stages. Never invent data, participants, approvals, measurements, analysis output, or citations to make the pipeline appear complete. Publishing or external submission requires the user's instruction.
