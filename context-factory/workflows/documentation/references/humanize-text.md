# Unslop

Read the passage for its audience and purpose. Preserve meaning, attribution, technical terminology, citations, numbers, voice, and necessary uncertainty. State mechanisms and observed results with concrete subjects, active verbs, familiar words, and consistent terms.

Remove vague attribution, inflated language, filler, repeated previews, forced contrasts, ambiguous pronouns, and decorative formatting that adds no information. Use headings, lists, and longer sentences when they help the reader. A prose pass does not establish factual correctness: verify claims and keep intended, implemented, and observed behavior distinct. For durable artifacts, follow [documentation](../SKILL.md).
