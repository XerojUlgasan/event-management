# Research

Identify the user's requested stage, approved scope, manuscript, evidence, method, institutional requirements, and deliverable. Enter at the current stage; preserve an approved project. Read the applicable references below before working. Use [documentation](../documentation/SKILL.md) for source authority, artifact preparation, recursive review, and format checks.

| Task | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| Shape a new idea, scope, architecture, or evaluation | [Design IT capstones](references/design-it-capstones.md) |
| Stress-test viability or a material proposal revision | [Validate IT capstones](references/validate-it-capstones.md) |
| Verify citations, extract or compare studies, synthesize literature, or review a draft | [Research desk](references/research-desk.md) |
| Coordinate several stages or a whole paper | [Research superpowers](references/research-superpowers.md) |
| Prepare or rehearse a defense or cross-examine slides | [Thesis examiner](references/thesis-examiner.md) |

For a full paper, establish or validate scope as needed, check sources and methods, draft or revise, review substance, polish prose, and inspect the final artifact. Apply the [recursive review](../documentation/references/recursive-review.md) to the whole manuscript and supporting material. Distinguish planned procedures from completed work and measured results. Keep source locations and access limits; never invent evidence or imply an independent review that did not occur. If evidence or a material decision is missing, record the gap and continue independent work. Defense and publication stages apply when requested.
