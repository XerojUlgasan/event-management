# Research desk

Verify citations and claim support, take faithful source notes, compare studies, synthesize related work, and review research drafts. Use for evidence-grounded research work and state plainly what could not be verified.

Use the [documentation agent](../../../agents/documentation.md) for scope and source authority. Select the requested mode; reference checking, synthesis, and referee review have different outputs.

## Verify and record evidence

- Read supplied sources directly when available. Use primary research, publisher records, DOI records, and official documentation to verify uncertain or current claims; follow applicable browsing requirements. A search snippet or generated bibliography does not establish a paper's findings.
- Check both bibliographic identity (title, authors, year, venue, identifier) and support for the precise claim. A real DOI does not establish that the cited work supports a sentence.
- Record source location, relevant finding, methods/sample/context, limitations, and the claim it can support. Separate direct quotation, paraphrase, and inference. Preserve qualifiers, populations, dates, and units.
- State access limits: metadata-only, abstract-only, supplied excerpt, or full text. Do not claim full-text verification from an abstract or paywalled listing. Do not invent missing identifiers, page numbers, quotations, or sources.
- For client evidence, preserve interview timestamps and distinguish observed statements from later scope decisions. An interview from one business does not establish demand across the Philippines.

## Choose the output

| Mode | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Reference audit | Citation/claim location, verified identity, degree of accessible support, mismatch or uncertainty, and a supported correction where available |
| Paper notes | Research question, context, method, relevant findings, limitations, source locations, and relevance to this project |
| Comparison | Shared comparison criteria, evidence from each source, limits on comparability, and a bounded conclusion |
| Related work | A synthesis organized by theme, method, or gap; explain agreements, differences, and relevance instead of listing one summary per paper |
| Referee review | Findings ordered by impact, each with location, evidence, consequence, and a practical revision; include strengths and limits when useful |
| Weighted decision | Agreed or explicitly provisional criteria and weights, evidence for scores, sensitivity to uncertain scores, and a recommendation with limitations |

Base synthesis on the user's sources and any additional research authorized or needed for the task. Keep added sources traceable. Verify citation and bibliography correspondence in both directions; do not silently retain an unsupported citation because its formatting is correct.

For a requested second opinion, explain whether it is a separate review pass or an independently obtained review. Do not claim an independent reviewer or multiple judges when only one review occurred. Delegation is optional and requires authorization under the active instructions.

## Close the loop

For review-only work, report findings without rewriting. For requested revisions, use [docs](../../documentation/references/docs.md) and recheck affected claims, methods, results, and dependent sections with the [recursive review](../../documentation/references/recursive-review.md). Route proposal viability questions to [validate-it-capstones](validate-it-capstones.md); polish the prose after the substantive issues are addressed.
