# Prevail Events Management interview summary

Recorded: 2026-10-28. Source: [full transcription](../transcriptions/full-transcription.md) of two Taglish recordings ([Video 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1x_fuphoWiaI8RzbCqP9tvwJaAGR7wP1a/view?usp=drive_link), 35:18; [Video 2](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LwBLv_4ysFUNDktVQoF3eg1ue7UIBPpM/view?usp=drive_link), 12:28). Times below refer to individual videos. Some speech is inaudible or unattributed; the interview is evidence, not detailed approval of every proposed feature.

## Interview findings

- Customers commonly ask about rates or location; staff gather event type, date and time, guest count, venue and requested services. Two people handle the page and quotations. (Video 1, 07:06–08:36; 15:31–16:34; 21:18–22:01)
- Accurate quotations for changing requirements are a challenge. Prevail prefers collecting requirements first; fixed-price packages for a defined guest count may coexist with custom quotations. Staff prepare tailored quotations and discuss changes with customers. (Video 1, 08:07–09:07; 16:55–17:51; 23:28–26:12)
- The interviewee confirmed that a person should take over when the AI cannot answer. Staff handle payment personally; the interview does not establish complete confirmation or later event rules. (Video 2, 00:22–01:47; Video 1, 00:51–01:58; 09:07–09:44)
- Staff are mostly on call and selected by availability. The interviewee welcomed manager-selected teams, availability responses and a checklist or timeline. Detailed assignment, status and notification rules were not agreed. (Video 1, 02:29–04:49; 27:41–28:32)
- A website, management system and staff app were discussed positively, without detailed acceptance of every workflow. Approved information and its maintenance need definition before an assistant can rely on it. (Video 1, 20:33–21:18; Video 2, 04:23–04:53; 06:52–07:43)
- The interviewee offered sample intake questions and terms; the recording does not establish that they were delivered. One year of support was discussed, but its detailed terms were not defined. (Video 1, 22:01–23:28; 29:21–29:42; Video 2, 07:43–10:23)

## Scope decisions recorded 2026-11-03

Prevail Events Management is the confirmed client. Clemora remains the product name. Intended scope includes both package-start and requirements-first inquiries, staff-prepared custom quotations, workforce management, factual AI-assisted customer service with human handoff, reports, and a managed past-events showcase. These decisions are separate from the interview and do not mean all details were approved there or implemented.

All payment responsibilities are outside the system. Equipment/inventory management and supplier management/coordination are outside scope. Staff delivery platform remains undecided. Custom package/service combination configuration, menus, policies, terms and AI knowledge maintenance are later work. See the root README's [product scope](../README.md#product-scope) and [current implementation](../README.md#current-implementation) for current boundaries and implementation status.

Confirm intake fields, quotation and approval rules, availability and booking confirmation, workforce operations, assistant sources and handoff ownership, and report/showcase details with the client before implementing dependent work. Meta integration and other channels require separate decisions; no integration was approved in the interview. (Video 2, 04:56–09:53)
