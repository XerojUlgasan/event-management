# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **System Administrator:** manages public package and service content, reviews customer requests, communicates with customers, prepares quotations, and manages event schedules.
- **Customer:** explores packages and services; submits an event request; and communicates with the administrator about the request and quotation.
- **Public visitor:** browses published event offerings and starts a planning inquiry.

## Product Purpose

Styled by AMAC is a web-based event-management product for presenting event offerings and coordinating customer requests through administration. It supports the flow from catalog discovery and inquiry through quotation and scheduling. Product scope and current implementation are owned by the [product reference](../../context-factory/business/product.md).

## Operating Context

Customers begin with a package or describe their requirements first. Administrators gather missing details, discuss custom packages and additions, prepare quotations and revisions, communicate through booking-linked conversations, and manage event schedules. An inquiry does not reserve a date or confirm a booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app includes public package and service catalogs; customer and administrator workspaces; booking requests and quotations; messaging; and event scheduling. Roles and permissions must follow the implemented access rules and the intended responsibilities in the [business identity reference](../../context-factory/business/identity.md). Payment, inventory, and supplier management are outside approved scope. Confirmation, date-hold, cancellation, and other undecided business policies must follow the open decisions recorded in the [product reference](../../context-factory/business/product.md), not be inferred from this brand or visual system.

## Brand Commitments

The visible website brand is **Styled by AMAC**. Use the supplied, unmodified logo at `packages/ui/src/assets/styled-by-amac.png`. The approved brand palette is charcoal, off-white, and taupe. The rebrand changes visual identity and naming; it does not change product scope or establish legal ownership.
